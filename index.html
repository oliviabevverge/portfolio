About Me

Science brain.
Builder's hands.

I started out in biomedical science at the University of Ottawa — fascinated by how living systems work. That curiosity led me to mechatronics engineering at Memorial University, where I could actually build systems that respond, adapt, and interface with the world.

I love working on problems that don't fit neatly into one discipline. Whether it's programming sensor logic, designing an enclosure in CAD, or coordinating a team through a real industry brief — I bring a scientific approach and a creative eye.

Outside of projects, I serve as VP Junior Academic for the MUN Engineering Society and work as a Resident Assistant. I'm fluent in both English and French.
